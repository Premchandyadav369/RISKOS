## 🚀 Track 1: Publish on SSRN (Recommended First Step — Immediate & Free)

**SSRN (Social Science Research Network / Elsevier)** is the gold standard preprint server for quantitative finance, financial engineering, and economics. Top hedge funds (AQR, Two Sigma, Citadel), asset managers (BlackRock), and universities (NYU Stern, Chicago, Oxford) publish their research on SSRN.

### Why SSRN First?
- **Immediate Acceptance**: Approved within 24–48 hours.
- **Permanent DOI**: Gives your paper an official, globally citable Digital Object Identifier (DOI).
- **Indexed on Google Scholar**: Automatically indexed so citations count toward your academic profile.
- **100% Free**: No submission or publication fees.

### Step-by-Step Submission:
1. Go to **[https://www.ssrn.com](https://www.ssrn.com)** and create a free author account.
2. Click **"Submit a Paper"** (in User Head / Dashboard).
3. **Paper Details**:

## 🚀 Track 2: Publish on arXiv (Quantitative Finance — `q-fin`)

**arXiv.org** (Cornell University) is the world's most prestigious open-access archive for physics, mathematics, computer science, and quantitative finance.

### Relevant arXiv Categories:
- **`q-fin.PM`** (Portfolio Management)
- **`q-fin.CP`** (Computational Finance)
- **`q-fin.RM`** (Risk Management)
- **`q-fin.TR`** (Trading and Market Microstructure)

### Step-by-Step Submission:
1. Go to **[https://arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org)** and log in.
2. Click **"START NEW SUBMISSION"**.
3. Select primary category: `q-fin.PM` or `q-fin.CP`.
4. Upload `RISKOS_RESEARCH_PAPER.tex` (or a `.zip` containing the `.tex` file and any figures).
5. arXiv compiles the LaTeX automatically into a verified arXiv preprint.
*(Note: If it's your first time submitting to `q-fin`, you may need an endorsement from an existing arXiv author in that category. If you don't have an endorsement yet, SSRN has no endorsement barrier and is accepted immediately!)*
