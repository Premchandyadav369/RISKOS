Model Sandbox 2: GARCH(1,1) Conditional Volatility Simulator

Bollerslev (1986) • Dynamic Stationarity
Layman Analogy — The Market Memory Effect

Volatility is sticky: a violent 2% crash today makes another big swing tomorrow far more likely than a quiet day. GARCH calculates this "memory effect" to tell you exactly when the storm has passed.

Quant Formulation — Conditional Heteroskedasticity
$$\sigma_t^2 = \omega + \alpha \epsilon_{t-1}^2 + \beta \sigma_{t-1}^2, \quad \alpha + \beta < 1$$

Requires covariance stationarity: if $\alpha + \beta \ge 1$, unconditional variance explodes to infinity.

Model Sandbox 4: Portfolio CVaR 99% & Extreme Value Distribution

Rockafellar-Uryasev • Tail Risk
Layman Analogy — Surviving the Worst-Case Day

VaR tells you: "On 99 out of 100 days, your losses won't exceed X." But what happens on that 1 remaining catastrophic day? CVaR (Conditional VaR) calculates the exact average devastation of that disaster day so your portfolio never goes bust.

Quant Formulation — Subadditive Coherent Risk
$$\text{CVaR}_\alpha = \frac{1}{1 - \alpha} \int_\alpha^1 \text{VaR}_u du = \mu + \frac{\phi(z_\alpha)}{1 - \alpha} \sigma$$

Unlike VaR, CVaR is convex and coherent, making it optimal for convex programming optimization.
