Desk Primer — How to Interpret Tail Risk & CVaR Optimization

Value at Risk (VaR 99%): The boundary of normal bad days — on 99 out of 100 days, your portfolio will not lose more than this amount. Expected Shortfall (CVaR 99%): The true crisis metric — the average loss expected when that 1% disaster strikes. CVaR Optimizer: Allocates capital to specifically minimize your loss during market crashes (rather than classical Markowitz which treats good gains as risk). Kupiec & Christoffersen: Basel III statistical tests verifying that your risk model is accurate and tail breaches do not clump together.

Desk Primer — How to Interpret Futures Spreads & Yield Curves

Kalman Filter Dynamic β: The real-time hedge ratio telling you how many shares of Asset B to short for every 1 share of Asset A you buy. Z-Score Signal: When Z > +2.0, the spread is abnormally wide (Short the spread). When Z < -2.0, the spread is abnormally narrow (Long the spread). OU Half-Life: The exact expected number of days for the spread to mean-revert back to equilibrium. Nelson-Siegel-Svensson & PCA: Fits continuous yield curves; 2s10s slope indicates steepener/flattener trades, and PC1/PC2/PC3 explain 99%+ of curve shifts.

Desk Primer — How to Interpret Order Flow & Microstructure

Level-2 DOM Ladder: The live queue of buyers (green) and sellers (red) waiting at each price level. Micro-Price: The true fair price weighted by bid/ask queue sizes; if bid size dominates, Micro-Price sits above Mid-Price, forecasting an upward tick. Order Flow Imbalance (OFI): Net volume buying pressure (+1.0 strong buyers, -1.0 strong sellers). VPIN Toxicity: Flags when aggressive institutional informed traders are active, warning market makers of imminent adverse selection. Almgren-Chriss: Slices large institutional orders to minimize market impact while managing volatility risk.

Desk Primer — How to Interpret Greeks & Volatility Surface

Delta (Δ) & Gamma (Γ): Delta is your directional exposure (and stock hedge ratio); Gamma is how fast your Delta changes when the stock moves. Vega & Theta (Θ): Vega measures sensitivity to volatility changes; Theta is the daily dollar decay you earn/pay over time. Cross-Greeks (Vanna, Volga, Charm): Vanna measures how your Delta shifts when volatility moves; Volga measures your exposure to Volatility Smile convexity; Charm is Delta decay per day. Delta-Hedging Simulator: Shows how continuous stock rebalancing replicates an option payoff and locks in theoretical PnL after transaction costs.

Desk Primer — How to Interpret Risk Parity & Brinson Attribution

Fractional Kelly: Mathematical sizing maximizing compound capital growth while preventing catastrophic drawdowns.
