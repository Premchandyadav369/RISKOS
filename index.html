Institutional Architecture

Six Quantitative Desks.
One Unified Terminal.

Engineered to institutional standards with rigorous mathematical formulations—from Kalman cointegration to Nelson-Siegel yield curves and cross-Greeks.

01

Market Intelligence & Regime Analytics

Surfaces macro shifts and volatility clustering across equity, rates, and commodities using time-varying volatility and latent Markov states.

Under the hood

  • EWMA & GARCH(1,1) Volatility — Maximum likelihood estimation of conditional variance $\sigma_t^2 = \omega + \alpha \epsilon_{t-1}^2 + \beta \sigma_{t-1}^2$ capturing volatility clustering and persistence.
  • 3-State Hidden Markov Model (HMM) — Unsupervised Baum-Welch training and Viterbi decoding identifying latent Bull, Bear, and Mean-Reverting Sideways regimes.
  • Ledoit-Wolf Covariance Shrinkage — Optimal analytical shrinkage toward constant correlation structure, preventing ill-conditioned sample covariance inversion.
02

Portfolio Risk Engine & CVaR Optimizer

Computes multi-horizon tail risk (VaR & Expected Shortfall), runs non-linear portfolio optimization, and validates models via Basel III backtesting.

Under the hood

  • Monte Carlo VaR & CVaR — 10,000 correlated path simulations computing 99% Conditional Value at Risk ($E[L \mid L > \text{VaR}_{99}]$).
  • CVaR Portfolio Optimization (SLSQP) — Non-linear Rockafellar-Uryasev formulation minimizing tail loss under institutional weight constraints.
  • Kupiec POF & Christoffersen Tests — Likelihood ratio tests verifying unconditional coverage and independence of tail breaches.
  • Macro Stress Testing — Simulates instantaneous rate spikes (+300bps), equity crashes (-40%), and credit contagion correlation breakdown.
03

Futures Spread & Yield Curve Rates Desk

Statistical arbitrage for calendar/commodity spreads using recursive Kalman filters and US Treasury yield curve parametric decomposition.

Under the hood

  • Kalman Filter Dynamic Hedge Ratio ($\beta_t$) — Online recursive state-space hedge estimation avoiding static OLS regression lag.
  • Ornstein-Uhlenbeck (OU) Mean Reversion — Analytical calibration of half-life ($\tau = \frac{\ln 2}{\theta}$) and equilibrium mean for mean-reverting futures pairs.
  • Nelson-Siegel-Svensson (NSS) Zero Curve — Continuous parametric yield curve fitting from 3M to 30Y tenors.
  • Yield Curve PCA Decomposition — Factor variance decomposition into Level (86.4%), Slope/2s10s (10.8%), and Curvature/Butterfly (2.8%).
04

Market Microstructure & Order Flow Desk

Analyzes Level-2 order book depth queues, order flow imbalance, informed trader toxicity, and optimal execution trajectories.

Under the hood

  • Order Flow Imbalance (OFI) — Real-time net volume pressure at the top of the limit order book predicting sub-second price moves.
  • Micro-Price Calculation — Fair price estimator incorporating bid-ask queue depth sizes ($P_{\text{micro}} = \frac{Q_b P_a + Q_a P_b}{Q_b + Q_a}$).
  • VPIN (Volume-Synchronized Probability of Toxicity) — Measures adverse selection risk and impending liquidity vacuums.
  • Almgren-Chriss Optimal Execution — Calculus of variations balancing linear/quadratic market impact against volatility risk.
05

Derivatives, Volatility Surface & Greeks Lab

European options analytical pricing, full 1st and 2nd order cross-greeks suite, and continuous discrete delta-hedging simulation.

Under the hood

  • 1st & 2nd Order Analytical Greeks — Exact Black-Scholes formulas for $\Delta$, $\Gamma$, $\text{Vega}$, $\Theta$, and $\rho$.
  • Cross-Greeks (Vanna, Volga, Charm) — Vanna ($\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S \partial \sigma}$), Volga ($\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial \sigma^2}$), and Charm ($\frac{\partial \Delta}{\partial t}$) for market-maker surface risk.
  • Volatility Smile / Skew Modeling — Parametric SVI / SABR curvature across strike moneyness ($0.8\times$ to $1.2\times$).
  • Discrete Delta-Hedging Simulator — Simulates daily dynamic delta replication PnL with slippage and transaction cost drag.
06

PnL Attribution & Risk Parity Allocation

Attribution of alpha into macroeconomic decisions and systematic portfolio sizing using equal risk contribution and Fractional Kelly.

Under the hood

  • Brinson-Fachler PnL Decomposition — Deconstructs excess returns into Sector Allocation, Asset Selection, and Interaction effects.
  • Equal Risk Contribution (Risk Parity) — Equalizes marginal risk contributions across assets ($w_i (\Sigma w)_i = \text{const}$).
  • Multi-Asset Fractional Kelly Criterion — Dynamic sizing ($F^* = \kappa \Sigma^{-1} \mu$) maximizing geometric growth while capping drawdown.
