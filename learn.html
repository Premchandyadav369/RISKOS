Dual Momentum & Volatility Targeting

Absolute Trend + Cross-Asset Relative Strength
TREND / MOMENTUM
Execution Invariant:
$$w_i(t) = \min\left(w_{\max}, \frac{\sigma_{\text{tgt}}}{\sigma_i(t)}\right) \cdot \mathbf{1}_{\{ R_i(12\text{M}) > R_f \}} \cdot \mathbf{1}_{\{ \text{Rank}(R_i) \le K \}}$$

Combines Antonacci absolute momentum (flight to cash/T-Bills if 12M return is negative) with cross-sectional relative strength. Volatility targeting dynamically rescales position size inverse to 20-day realized volatility.

Kalman Filter Cointegration Stat-Arb

Dynamic State-Space Pairs & Basket Mean Reversion
STAT ARB / PAIRS
State-Space Model:
$$y_t = \beta_t x_t + \epsilon_t, \quad \beta_t = \beta_{t-1} + \omega_t, \quad z_t = \frac{\epsilon_t - \mu_\epsilon}{\sigma_\epsilon}$$

Replaces static linear regression with recursive Kalman filtering to estimate time-varying hedge ratios $\beta_t$. Triggers mean-reversion long/short entry when spread z-score exceeds $\pm 2.0\sigma$, exiting at $z=0$.

Entry: |z| ≥ 2.0σ Half-Life: ~8.4D Market-Neutral: β = 0

Avellaneda-Stoikov HFT Market Making

Optimal Inventory Risk Skew & Spread Capture
HFT / MARKET MAKING
Reservation Price:
$$r(s, q, t) = s - q \gamma \sigma^2 (T - t), \quad \delta^a + \delta^b = \frac{2}{\gamma} \ln\left(1 + \frac{\gamma}{\kappa}\right)$$

High-frequency market making model where the quoting engine continuously shades bid and ask distances based on active inventory $q$. Long inventory skews quotes downward to attract aggressive sells and deter buys.

Risk Aversion: γ = 0.1 Latency: < 500μs Max Inventory: ±500

0DTE Options Gamma Exposure (GEX)

Market Maker Hedging Reflexivity & Strike Pinning
DERIVATIVES / GEX
Net Market GEX:
$$\text{Net GEX} = \sum_i \Gamma_i \cdot \text{OI}_{i, \text{call}} \cdot S - \sum_j \Gamma_j \cdot \text{OI}_{j, \text{put}} \cdot S$$

Tracks dealer hedging constraints across weekly expiry options chains. High positive Net GEX acts as a volatility sponge pinning price to maximum open interest strikes; negative GEX triggers violent trending breakouts.

Flip Strike: K_zero Expiry: 0DTE / Weekly Reflexivity: 85%

Cash & Carry Futures Basis Roll

Spot-Futures Yield Capture & Term Structure Arbitrage
CARRY / COMMODITIES
Annualized Basis APR:
$$\text{APR} = \left( \frac{F_t - S_t}{S_t} \right) \times \frac{365}{T - t} - (r_{\text{borrow}} + \text{Carry Cost})$$

Capitalizes on futures basis contango/backwardation spreads. Buys spot and shorts near-month futures when basis yield exceeds money market financing costs, capturing mechanical price convergence at contract expiry.

Basis APR: 8.5% - 14% Roll Cadence: Monthly Delta Risk: ~0

Hawkes Point Process & Order Cascades

Self-Exciting Flow & Flash Crash Early Warning
ORDER FLOW / OFI
Conditional Intensity:
$$\lambda(t) = \mu + \sum_{t_i < t} \alpha e^{-\beta(t - t_i)}, \quad \eta = \frac{\alpha}{\beta} \quad (\eta \ge 1 \implies \text{Runaway Crash})$$

Models mutual excitation of aggressive market orders across limit order book queues. When the branching ratio $\eta = \alpha/\beta$ nears 1.0, order arrival clusters create severe liquidity holes, triggering autonomous defensive de-risking.

Branching Ratio: η < 0.85 Criticality: η ≥ 1.0 False Alarms: < 4%
